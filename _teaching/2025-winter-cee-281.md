I was a teaching assistant for this graduate-level course in the winter quarters of 2023, 2024, and 2025 (about 20 students), taught by Professor Ronaldo I. Borja. I held office hours every other week, graded problem sets, and led supplementary computing sessions. These covered two-dimensional finite element solutions to Poisson's equation, fluid seepage around a pile, and cantilever beam bending with different element types, including how to avoid volumetric locking near the incompressible limit.
